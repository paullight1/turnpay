# Ghana bank logo sources

The unmodified logos in this directory identify receiving banks in the TrustPay bank selector. Bank names and logos remain the trademarks of their respective owners; inclusion does not imply an affiliation or endorsement. These assets are not covered by a blanket MIT license for the application.

The bank-name directory is sourced from the Bank of Ghana registered-banks list:
https://www.bog.gov.gh/supervision-regulation/registered-institutions/banks/

Twenty PNGs are sourced from GIRSAL's public partner-bank directory and Ghana Cares page. GIRSAL does not publish an explicit media reuse license. Original source URLs are retained below; no logo artwork was modified.
