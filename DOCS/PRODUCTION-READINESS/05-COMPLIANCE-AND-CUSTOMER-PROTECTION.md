# 05 — Compliance and Customer Protection

## Goal

Meet applicable Nigerian financial, privacy, KYC/AML, consumer-protection, and record-keeping obligations before handling real funds.

## Owner

Compliance officer, legal counsel, and risk owner.

## Steps

- [ ] Confirm the legal entity, licensing model, regulated partners, permitted products, countries, currencies, and transaction limits with counsel.
- [ ] Define KYC tiers, required documents, verification outcomes, sanctions/PEP screening, enhanced due diligence, and re-verification rules.
- [ ] Define AML monitoring: velocity limits, unusual patterns, structuring, rapid movement, high-risk countries, blocked users, case review, and escalation.
- [ ] Define suspicious-activity reporting, record retention, legal holds, staff access, evidence export, and regulator response procedures.
- [ ] Publish terms of service, privacy notice, fee schedule, exchange-rate disclosures, refund/reversal policy, complaints process, and support SLAs.
- [ ] Verify consent, data minimization, encryption, access reviews, deletion/retention behavior, and cross-border data-processing agreements.
- [ ] Train support, finance, operations, and engineering staff; record completion and annual refresh dates.
- [ ] Obtain written legal/compliance sign-off for launch scope and customer-facing disclosures.

## Evidence required

Legal opinion, license/partner records, KYC/AML policy, screening configuration, sample cases, privacy review, published policies, training records, and compliance sign-off.

## Exit criteria

The launch product, limits, jurisdictions, data flows, monitoring, reporting, and customer remedies are approved in writing by compliance and counsel.
