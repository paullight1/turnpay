# Direct-account exchange V1

Approved direction: the owner requested implementation of direct payouts in both directions after the 4 November discussion. New NGN→GHS orders collect Ghana bank/MoMo details; new GHS→NGN orders collect a Nigerian bank name, ten-digit account number and beneficiary name. Dedicated collection, customer PIN/proof, admin statement verification and assigned-operator external payment remain. No provider transfer is initiated by recording evidence.

Use the current manual order aggregate and private evidence infrastructure. New reverse orders use a server-owned ADMIN_EXTERNAL workflow and a RECORD_PAYOUT_SENT action requiring the exact frozen amount, unique reference and private evidence. Existing ADMIN_CEDIS and historical WALLET/LEGACY orders retain their recorded contracts and retry recovery.

Owner update: direct exchanges have **no application per-transaction or daily limit**. Do not gate the product on wallet balances/limits or new exchange-limit settings. Remove the old 10,000,000 source-amount cap. Valid positive two-decimal amounts, rates and computed payouts must fit the existing PostgreSQL Decimal(15,2)/Decimal(18,6) storage exactly; rejecting unrepresentable input is precision validation, not a commercial cap. Verified Tier 1 or higher is still required. Keep the customer-row lock for duplicate recovery and fresh eligibility, not allowance reservation. Preserve committed retries and existing funded obligations when new creation is disabled.

The Flutter receiving form uses a Nigerian bank-name field and ten-digit account validation with honest manual beneficiary verification. External exchange authorization checks KYC and suspension without applying wallet limits. Historical attempts retain KYC/PIN and immutable recorded terms without applying new-order rules to replay. The admin exposes clear confirm-receipt, start-transfer and record-payment actions in each currency. Never mark successful before actual external payment is recorded.

Alternatives considered: retaining wallet settlement fails the owner's cash-out requirement; enabling provider automation adds merchant/integration acceptance work. Manual external settlement through the existing order queue is the approved V1 approach.

No deployment, genuine payment, identity decision, production configuration mutation is part of this implementation. Test against disposable PostgreSQL and synthetic provider/storage boundaries. Required evidence includes duplicate/concurrent creation, large amounts and repeated orders with no policy caps, no wallet movement, claim ownership, exact payout evidence, legacy replay, both client directions, error recovery and small/large text layouts.

Review hardening: old app builds must not create new reverse-bank orders with their Ghana-only bank form. Require deliveryPolicyVersion=2 for new GHS orders after replay lookup; the updated client preserves legacy journal bodies and keys. Use database time for createdAt and quote expiry. Direct orders are excluded from wallet daily spending as well as from wallet limits at creation. Keep suspended-account checks, show pending retry forms even when new orders are disabled, and do not revalidate read-only historical destinations against the new Nigerian-account format. Both ADMIN_CEDIS and ADMIN_EXTERNAL can complete an already-sent supervised handover only with its persisted exact-amount evidence/event.
